About Off-Grid-Site

What is Off-Grid-Site?

Off-Grid-Site is a device that stores HTML websites and serves them on the go. It generates its own SSID and mobile hotspot, so once a user connects, whatever HTML code you add is hosted.

Why?

I wanted a device that could serve a webpage to other people, mainly as a fun prank while on the bus or in a coffee shop. Fairly childish, I know!

What can it be used for?

It really is down to your imagination! You can, relatively speaking, host anything and serve it to anyone in a local area. 
Some uses, maybe...
Promotion: Serve a promo webpage at a convention or meet-up. 
Prank: Just for fun, prank people at a bar or train station.
Sell yourself: Host your CV at a job interview.
Education: Host a lesson for children to access in the classroom.
Off-grid message board: Serve a message board at festivals or concerts.

The uses are endless.

Who can see the hosted pages?

Anyone locally who joins the network will be directed automatically to your hosted website. 

What does local mean?
• Indoors (with walls and floors): Around 10 to 20 meters (30 to 65 feet) before signal degradation or dropped connections occur.
• Outdoors (standard / reliable connection): Up to 30 to 100 meters (100 to 330 feet) with a standard onboard PCB antenna.
Instructions

Setup the device.

1. Power the device with a powerbank, phone charger or laptop USB.
2. From a mobile phone or PC, join the network created by Off-Grid-Site. On first boot, it will show up as "Off-Grid-Site" but you can change it in the settings to whatever you like.
3. You should be directed to the Off-Grid-Site home page. From there, you can access the admin settings and upload your own HTML site. If you are not automatically taken to the site, open your browser and go to http://wifi.lan/admin

Off-Grid-Site can store HTML files in its onboard memory, but if your Off-Grid-Site has a micro sd card slot, you have the ability to store bigger files. 
It is advised to add an admin password to prevent anyone from accessing the settings page.

How to make a webpage?

I have designed a few sample sites that can be uploaded to the device, so you have an idea of how things work and can instantly get started hosting content.
There are many free HTML editors on the internet that allow you to make simple webpages and sites.
I made a website creator (Sightwright) on GitHub that helps anyone design websites and pages; in fact, it is what this site was designed with. All you need is a free GitHub account! Not only will your HTML code be available to download for use with Off-Grid-Site, but it can also be viewed by anyone on the internet if you want.
